# Brand

## MEASURED doctrine

- Evidence over assertion.
- Precision over spectacle.
- Reduction over addition.
- Engineering before marketing.
- Every number has provenance.
- Every recommendation has a path to production.
- Every claimed saving ends at the bill.

The site should feel technical, calm, financially literate, exact, high-trust, and intentionally restrained. It must not feel like a generic SaaS template, AI startup, FinOps dashboard clone, agency template, crypto site, or imitation of another technology brand.

Supplied references also establish an interlocked HKG monogram, blue/green identity accents, and metallic/space campaign artwork. Their website status is deliberately bounded:

The existing semantic relationship remains authoritative: blue communicates signal/action, while green is reserved for verified or measured outcomes. Do not sample new colours from raster references, create blue-green gradients, make all branding blue, or turn verified green into a generic action colour. The supplied campaign references do not redesign the MEASURED system.

## Voice

Writing should be concrete, concise, technical without unnecessary jargon, financially precise, calm, and proof-oriented. State the evidence, scope, dependency, or unknown behind a claim.

Avoid generic marketing patterns including:

- “unlock unprecedented efficiency”;
- “supercharge your FinOps journey”;
- “revolutionary savings”;
- “transform your cloud economics”;
- vague “AI-powered” claims.

Do not fabricate testimonials, logos, customers, security certifications, metrics, outcomes, or guarantees.

## Positioning principle

**Recommendations are not savings. Shipped changes are.**

This is a working strategic statement, not confirmed registered marketing copy.
